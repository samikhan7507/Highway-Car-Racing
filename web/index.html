HOW TO PLAY

  

  Goal: finish 2 laps of the city circuit ahead of 7 AI racers. Overtake for bonus points; finishing position and time give the biggest score.

  Controls: W/↑ gas · S/↓ brake/reverse · A,D/←→ steer · SPACE nitro · B handbrake · C change camera (chase / far / hood / cinematic) · H help · P pause. Phone: on-screen buttons (gas is automatic).

  Guide arrows: coloured arrows on the road show the racing line — green = speed is safe, orange = slow down, red = brake now. The top banner announces the next turn, its distance and safe speed.

  Track: city streets → long bridge over the water → rough gravel road (slow, bumpy, no rails) → tight hairpins and a chicane.

  Nitro: recharges slowly and when you overtake. The camera looks into corners and leans with the car.

  Records: after the race you see the results table and the high-score leaderboard.
